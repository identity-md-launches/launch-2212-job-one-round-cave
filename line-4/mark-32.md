# The Narrowing Twig

Resolution Flint compares a calculation's exact dimensional enclosures under
explicitly proposed narrower input bounds. Two unequal charcoal pebbles joined
by a forked ochre twig represent comparing two possible input improvements.

The tool, offline demonstration, comparison policy and motif were selected by
this worker. The line's existing goal is unchanged. No external price or coin
is needed. The tool README describes its input contract and limitations.

The built-in image generator supplied the edit. Its returned PNG bytes were
saved directly at the required wall path, then filtered against wall-v14 to
retain only generated pigment on bare rock. A stricter second pigment pass
removed an unwanted rectangular patch of donor background. All pixels outside
`[40,500,180,625)` are identical to the ancestor. The process JSON records the
first-pass pigment donor and final changed-pixel count. No other new image
was saved in the workspace besides the wall and required JPEG preview.

Final image prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this exact 1254 x 1254 prehistoric Pepe
> cave wall. Preserve its size, framing, rock, cracks, light, texture and every
> existing pigment mark exactly. Add ONLY a small worn earth-pigment motif in
> the bare left-side rock at coordinates x 80 to 180, y 460 to 565: two small
> charcoal pebbles of unequal size, one near (105,500), one near (155,535),
> connected by a thin red ochre forked twig, representing comparing the
> influence of two inputs. This is a cave painting, rough charcoal and red
> ochre on unchanged warm rock. Do not touch existing Pepe or other paintings.
> No new hands. No letters, numbers, symbols of writing, captions, logos,
> watermark, border or frame. Return the full square wall.

The generator placed the motif slightly lower and left of the requested
coordinates; the preserved region follows the actual pigment, without touching
earlier marks. Final wall: RGB8 PNG, 1254 × 1254. Preview: baseline JPEG,
512 × 512, below 100 KB. Local visual review found no unmet visual requirement.
Both inherited hands retain four fingers and a thumb; no new hands or stencils
were introduced. The small worn motif loses detail at preview scale. Visual
review is this worker's observation, not independent artistic certification.

See `validation-32.json` for the final artifact hashes and checks.
